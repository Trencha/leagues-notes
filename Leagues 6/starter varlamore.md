We don't unlock Karamja until a little later this time, so Karamja is its own file rather than being included in starters like it was in previous Leagues

This file is currently WIP as the updated Varlamore and global task lists are yet to be revealed, so I'm basing it on the global tasks and Varlamore tasks from the previous League.

In the last League, Auburnvale and the Tlati Rainforest had not yet been revealed, so there will probably be a bunch of new tasks in these areas that are yet to be revealed.

Assuming we get an infinite use teleport back to Civitas akin to the free Lumbridge teleport from earlier Leagues, we have easy travel access to the following locations by going back to Civitas and taking the Quetzal:

- Aldarin
- Auburnvale
- Quetzalcali Gorge
- Sunset Coast
- Hunter Guild
- Tal Teklan

This means that the task order is more fluid than usual - any section that starts with taking a Quetzal can pretty much be done in any order.

# Yama's Lair

- Claim items from the Leagues tutor (and pets if you want)
- Consider training Agility unless you're taking Barbarian Gathering or Hotfoot
  - Depends on how good the Agility exp is, currently unknown
  - Also depends on if your first region choice is going to be one that has a low level course for you to train on

# Civitas Illa Fortis

- Turn on Prayer
- Pet Renu
- Head to the Cothon (harbour area)
- On the way there, pickpocket a Citizen until you have 28 coin pouches
- Open the coin pouches
- By a banana from a trader crewmember: Pot, Bucket, Shears, Banana and any basic items you lack (Chisel, Knife, Hammer, Tinderbox)
  - Optional: also buy pot, bucket, shears, chisel, knife. You can get these from the General Store too for cheaper, but they're faster to get here
- Eat the banana
- Go to the pub between the Cothon and the Bazaar and buy some Stew (20gp)
- Head south of the pub
- Drink from the bird bath
- Pet Xolo
- Bask at the Shrine of Ralos (probably a task)
- Head west
- Give Oli the stew (in the courtyard, south of the bank, west of the clothing store)
- Visit Death's Domain (north of the bank)
- Dance in a graveyard (not sure if the little area around Death's Domain counts, or if this will be a task this time)
- Exit Civitas through the west gate

# Heading west from Civitas

- Pick some wheat
- Cry in a wheat field
- Kill an imp (there is a spawn in the wheat field and a spawn in the buffalo pen)
- Scatter its ashes
- Kill a buffalo (they have a max hit of 2 and have 20 HP but they have negative defences, might need to safespot it over the fence)
- Bury its bones (if feeding bones to a dog is a task, feed them to Chiribaya instead)
- Pet Chiribaya (surely this will be a task?)
- Buy a rake
- Rake a farming patch
- Milk the dairy buffalo
- Shear an alpaca (keep hold of the wool)
- Head west to the mine south-east of Cam Torum, chopping trees as you go for Woodcutting exp
  - Burn a log
  - Fletch a log into arrow shafts
- Mine 5 tin and 5 copper
  - Note: these are the only Tin rocks in Varlamore, however there are Copper rocks elsewhere in Varlamore
  - If you want, you can mine more, to 15 Mining, and then mine some Iron too
  - Mining 50 Iron is a task
  - Probably not a good idea to mine much now if you plan on taking Hotfoot, better to wait to optimise Smithing exp
- Enter Cam Torum (note: Perilous Moons is autocompleted)
- Bank
- Buy a Steel Pickaxe if you can spare the cash
- Buy anything you need from the Cam Torum shops
- Smith your ores into bars at the furnace
- Make a Bronze Full Helm and Bronze Plateskirt if possible (7 and 16 Smithing respectively)
- Make an Iron Bar if possible
- Make 150 Iron Arrowtips if possible (20 Smithing, so probably not)
- Do any tasks in Cam Torum that are yet to be revealed
- Cast Home Teleport from the spellbook

# Quetzalcali Gorge

- Travel to Quetzalcali Gorge via Quetzal
- Bank
- Spin your wool that you got from an alpaca on the spinning wheel
- Do any other tasks in the area

# Tlati Rainforest

Note: the main point of this is to buy Fishing equipment, so you can skip this if you have Barbarian Gathering
- Travel to Tal Teklan via Quetzal
- Optional: buy runes and ammo
- Bank
- Smith your stuff if you haven't already
- Take the rowboat to Kastori
- Buy fishing supplies (mainly fishing rods, bait and feathers for now)
- Tlati Rainforest tasks (yet to be revealed)

# South of Civitas Illa Fortis

- Teleport back to Civitas
- Exit Civitas through the gate south of the bazaar
- Chop trees as you go for Woodcutting, Firemaking and/or Fletching exp
- Pick sweetcorn
- Cry in a wheat field if you didn't already
- Pick wheat
- Make flour in the windmill
- Head east and slightly south, to the coast
- Catch a Shrimp
- Cook a shrimp
- Burn a shrimp
- Keep fishing until 5
- Switch to the Rod spot
  - Where do we get the bait from if not taking Barbarian Gathering? May need to go to Kastori or Sunset Coast first
- Keep fishing until 15 Fishing and 25 Sardines
- Switch back to the Shrimp spot until you catch an Anchovy
- Cook your stuff

# Auburnvale

- Travel to Auburnvale via Quetzal
- Chop a tree
- Convert the logs into a plank at the Sawmill for (100gp)
- Auburnvale tasks (yet to be revealed)

# Aldarin

Once you have 2000 gold:
- Travel to Civitas
- Buy a house
- Travel to the Sunset Coast via Quetzal
- Take the boat to Aldarin
- Enter your house
- Make a Parlour
- Aldarin tasks (yet to be revealed)

# Ralos' Rise

- Travel to the Teomat via Quetzel
- Head north and kill an Icefiend
