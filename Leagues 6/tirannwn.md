# Tirannwn

## Prifddinas

- If you have 80 Hunter, keep a net on you at all times in case you see an impling
- Bank for: Sweetcorn seeds, axe, combat gear
- Go upstairs and safespot elves until you get a teleport crystal (charged or uncharged), then go back down
- Grab the Memoriam Crystal from the bush near the Tower of Voices, west of the Archery Range
- Thieve a Tiara from the Silver Stall (50 Thieving)
- Thieve a Diamond from the Gem Stall (75 Thieving)
- Plant some Sweetcorn if you have any seeds for it (20 Farming)
- Chop 100 Teaks (35 Woodcutting) (drop the logs unless you have a relic that lets you keep them)
- Go up the tower and ring the two bells, then go back down and run to the south-east bank
- Kill a rabbit and cook it (should be plenty around the south-east bank)
- Bank for: Mining gear, money, teleport crystal
- Kill Zalcano
  - Optional: kill it 50 times and go for the combat achievements
- Enter the Traeharn mine
  - Mine 25 gold (40 Mining)
  - Mine 200 Soft Clay (70 Mining)
- Go upstairs in the south-east corner and ring the two bells, then go back down
- Head towards the south-west corner
  - On your way past the weapon and armour stores, buy and equip full Mith and Addy if you haven't done those tasks yet
- Go upstairs and ring the two bells, then come back down
- Enter the Slayer dungeon
  - Kill a Bloodveld (50 Slayer)
  - Grab the Memoriam Crystal in a crystal outcrop between the Bloodvelds and Kurasks
  - Kill a Kurask (70 Slayer)
  - Kill a Moss Giant
  - Kill a Waterfiend
  - Kill a Nechryael (80 Slayer)
  - Kill a Dark Beast (90 Slayer)
- Leave the dungeon
- Head towards the Gauntlet
- Move your house to Priff (70 Construction)
- Talk to Ilfeen (near the house portal)
- Get Eluned to charge your teleport crystal for 250gp (north of the dye shop)
- Buy and equip a Crystal Crown if you have enough money
- Grab the memoriam crystal in the cooking pots in Tatie's house (south of the Magic Trees)
- Chop 50 Magic Logs (75 Woodcutting)
- Complete the Gauntlet
  - Optional: farm it as many times as you like
- Go upstairs and ring the last two bells
- Exit via the west gate
- Charter a ship to Port Tyras

## Lletya

- Use your Crystal Seed to teleport to Lletya
- Use the bank
- Plant a Papaya Tree if you have one
- Exit Lletya
- Hop over the tripwire until successful
- Head west (maybe requires 56 Agility)
- Pick up and drop the whiteberries 10 times

## Prifddinas round 2

- Teleport back to Priffdinas
- Head north
- Bank for: axe, box traps, combat gear (including an Anti-Dragon Shield if using melee)
- Do the Agility course (75 Agility) until 50 laps and 1:10 or less completion
  - Grab the memoriam crystal from the rock before climbing the tree
- Exit Priff through the north gate
- Catch 300 Red Chins
- Kill the "rabbit" and equip the Crystal Grail
- Chop 100 Maples
- Kill the Black Dragon at Mynydd
- Teleport back to Prif and add all the memoriam crystals to the memoriam device
